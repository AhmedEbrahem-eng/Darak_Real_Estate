## 2025-05-15 - [HIGH] Unprotected Administrative and BOLA Vulnerabilities in Tour Management
**Vulnerability:** Several administrative routes (reports, amenities, dashboard) were publicly accessible without authentication or 'admin' role checks. Additionally, tour approval and decline endpoints lacked ownership verification, allowing any authenticated user (or even unauthenticated users initially) to approve/decline tours for properties they didn't own (BOLA).
**Learning:** The application used a mix of protected and unprotected routes even within the same functional groups. Some routes were mistakenly left outside of middleware groups or didn't have specific authorization checks in the controller, relying only on the presence of a route.
**Prevention:** Always group administrative routes under a strict middleware stack (`auth:sanctum`, `admin`). For resources with ownership (like properties and tours), implement explicit BOLA checks in the controller or repository to verify the authenticated user's relationship to the resource.
